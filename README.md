## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Heroes of Lust and Magic**. This trainer enhances party progression and reduces repetitive management during the campaign. It supports character development, ability unlocking, relic collection, resource control, and combat assistance while you explore fractured realms, recruit companions, confront rival factions, and uncover the mystery of the vanished goddess Amura.

**Target Audience:** Players who want faster party development, easier relic and ability progression, reduced combat repetition, or a more relaxed way to experience the game's fantasy campaign and adult-themed story.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the normal game interface to travel between the fractured realms and their locations. Activate trainer options after entering a playable section rather than at the title screen or during loading.

### Tools & Inventory

Relics and other progression items are managed through the game's party and character interfaces. Enable resource or upgrade options before purchasing improvements, then refresh the relevant screen to confirm the changes.

### World & Environment

The campaign moves through cursed frontiers, imperial capitals, eastern strongholds, ruined wastelands, abyssal domains, and machine-built cities. Recruit companions, manage relationships with rival factions, and use progression options to prepare the party before entering difficult areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Build a party around the combat roles of recruited companions and improve their abilities as battles become more demanding. Health, combat-resource, party-power, and instant-defeat options should be activated only after combat has started and disabled before leaving the encounter.
